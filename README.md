# Squads

<img width="1005" height="513" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e63cb1b6-a9b0-46ec-89cf-bd4d4f09a4fd" />

- Secret Squad III: Dig up rocks, push rocks, throw rocks, encase buildings in rock. Mechs are burrowers and fliers, meaning rocks don't impede their movement.
- Secret Squad IV: Apply the Gunk status effect and benefit from it. Create various kinds of goos and nurture them to play as your very own goo boss. Feed gunk to spiderlings and lay traps.
- Ancient Draconids: Weapons have different effects in melee and at range. Eat Vek and spew their remains.
- Eldritch Automatons: Position your mechs carefully to expose your pilots to the Insanity status effect, which alters their weapons. Deploy an army of tentacles and brainwash Vek.
- Gurren Lagann: Boost your entire team and manage your ammunition carefully. Do the impossible!

# Enemies
- Blob Pack: adds more gooey enemies to the game, including the Titanic Goo, a boss that occupies four tiles.
- Omega Vek: adds variants of Alpha Vek, with configurable spawn chances. Omega Vek exist for vanilla, Bots & Bugs, and Vextra.
- Prefixed Vek: all enemies can now receive a prefix, which changes their stats, weapons, or gives a random buff. Over 30 prefixes. See mod options for explanations of each prefix or to disable them.
- Hunting Missions: adds four missions that have you hunt a special Vek with unique mechanics.
- Rock Leader: a joke mod based on the existence of a boss-colored rock in the files. It's a boss that buffs other Vek.
- Rogue Mechs: adds a single mission that has you face three Vek-controlled mechs. Not only do the mechs come from a pool of 12, they also have different stats and weapons based on the current island.
- Alt Final Missions: adds three final mission variants, including a multitile boss, the Hive Queen.

# Gameplay Modifiers
- Permanent Psions: Psions cannot spawn, and a Psion effect of your choice is permanently active. Makes for fun challenge runs. With Vextra, you can have two kinds of effects active at once.
- Adverse Conditions: at the start of each island, choose a negative effect to apply to the rest of your run.
- Pods+: set the odds for each kind of reward to appear in Time Pods, including rewards vanilla does not offer. Can be used to make the game easier or harder.

# Other
- Cyborg Weapons: Adds various weapons for Cyborg mechs.
- Spire Slayers: Adds six pilots from Slay the Spire and Slay the Spire 2.

# Pokemon
<img width="988" height="516" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d3e222f5-ebe2-43c1-b2ac-3cfe955d3ec8" />

The Pokemon expansion, featuring eight squads, five mechs that don't have a squad, several new missions and bosses, achievements, and a lot more.
Most mechs from this mod evolve, gaining weapons and/or changing graphics based on pilot level.
- Play as the classic starters from Kanto.
- Defeat legendary Pokemon to unlock them.
- Try to raise a Magikarp.
- Choose which form of Eevee (and others) to use.
- Discover Mega Evolution, the ultimate form of some Pokemon.
- Play as Missingno, the famous glich Pokemon, and use randomly-generated weapons that change every turn.
- Battle God, and play as God, featuring the first-ever weapon with four upgrades.
